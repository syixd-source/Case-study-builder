# Sample intake (fictional project)

This is a made-up project showing the level of detail the skill asks for. Replace every value with your own.

Project: Habit tracker for busy parents (concept)
Destinations: Personal portfolio site and Dribbble
Role: Solo UX and product designer
Tools: Figma
Dates: March 2026
Testing: 6 participants in moderated sessions. 4 of 6 finished setup without help.
Status: Concept

Assets provided:
- Logo: transparent PNG, mark only
- Screens: 24 PNG exports, named by flow (onboarding, today, habits, reports, settings)
- Design guide: accent #2F6BFF for actions, alert #D93025 for errors only, body typeface Inter, app typeface Nunito
- Photos: header image supplied; photo rights not yet confirmed, so do not publish a photo of a real person until they are
- Credit on closing page: name and role supplied

Expected output, in order:
01-cover, 02-overview, 03-problem, 04-persona (marked illustrative), 05-testing, 06-process, 07-structure, ... 19-closing
Each segment as 2x PNG, plus the PDF and self-contained HTML, zipped under the project name. Export sizes checked against Dribbble's current rules before export.

Gaps to flag before publishing:
- Any statistic in the problem segment needs a source, or it should be softened.
